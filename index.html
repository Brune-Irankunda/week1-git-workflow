Delicious Pancakes Recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder

Preparation

  1. Mix the flour, sugar, and baking powder.
  2. Add the eggs and milk.
  3. Mix until the batter is smooth.
  4. Cook the pancakes in a hot pan.
  5. Serve and enjoy.

Cooking time: 20 minutes

Servings: 4 people
